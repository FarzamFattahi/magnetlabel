# Example image provenance

`coffee.png` is the 600 × 400 coffee photograph distributed by scikit-image. The photographer is **Rachel Michetti**, courtesy of **Pikolo Espresso Bar**. Scikit-image documents the photograph as **CC0, with no copyright restrictions**.

- [Dataset description and license](https://scikit-image.org/docs/stable/api/skimage.data#skimage.data.coffee)
- [Pinned source file, scikit-image v0.19.3](https://github.com/scikit-image/scikit-image/blob/v0.19.3/skimage/data/coffee.png)
- [CC0 terms](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/)

Downloaded on 2 October 2026 for real-photograph interaction testing. The image is not a human-annotated benchmark. Masks produced by the example scripts are algorithm output, not ground truth. The three leaf images produced by the app's sample button are original procedural illustrations.

## Detection example: Chelsea

`chelsea.png` is the 451 × 300 Chelsea cat photograph by **Stefan van der Walt**, released as **CC0**. Downloaded on 3 October 2026.

- [Official description and license](https://scikit-image.org/docs/0.25.x/api/skimage.data.html#skimage.data.chelsea)
- [Pinned source, scikit-image v0.19.3](https://github.com/scikit-image/scikit-image/blob/v0.19.3/skimage/data/chelsea.png)

Detection screenshots and `detection-boxes.json` contain illustrative manually drawn visible-object rectangles for coffee and Chelsea. They are demonstrations, not expert ground truth. The photographs are cropped; the cat box covers the visible animal.
